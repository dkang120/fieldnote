A bottle's first day

Most cologne is made months before anyone buys it. Ours starts when you click order. Here's what happens next.

  1. Your order

    The formula is written down

    Your scent, size, name and city go onto a bench card with a formula code, so it can be remade exactly, years from now.

  2. 10:00 / 15:00

    Weighed by hand

    Two bench sessions every weekday. We weigh fragrance concentrate into perfumer's alcohol on a scale accurate to a hundredth of a gram.

  3. + 1 hour

    Mixed and filled

    The blend is stirred, filled into heavy glass, and the atomizer is crimped on so it can't leak.

  4. + 4 hours

    Rested and checked

    Every batch rests, then we check it for clarity and smell it against the master sample. We keep a small retained sample from each batch for a year.

  5. Same day

    Labeled for you

    Your label prints with your name, your city and the day it was made. Then it's boxed with a vial of another house scent.

  6. Next weekday

    On its way

    Order now and we mix it . It ships by ground.

What's inside

Eau de parfum at 15 to 18 percent fragrance concentrate, blended into perfumer's alcohol.

Our concentrates come from a fragrance house that certifies every accord to IFRA safety standards. A full ingredient list is printed on each box and listed on every product page.

Questions

When will my order ship?

Orders placed on a weekday before 3pm Pacific are mixed the same day. Everything ships the next weekday by ground, usually arriving in 2 to 5 days.

Why ground shipping only?

Cologne is alcohol-based, so it can't fly on most carriers. Ground is the safe, legal way to send it.

Does fresh-made fragrance last longer?

We won't promise that. What we can promise is that your bottle never sat in a warehouse, and the date on the label proves it.

Can I change the name after ordering?

Yes, until your bottle is mixed. Reply to your order email before the bench session and we'll update it.
