How fresh is fresh?

Your bottle is mixed at the next bench session after you order: weekdays, with a 3pm Pacific cutoff. The date on your label is the day it was made, not the day it left a warehouse.

Shipping and returns

Free ground shipping on orders over $75. Because every bottle carries your name, we can't restock it, but if a scent isn't right we'll remake it once in another house scent.
